4.1 · Evidence — the intellectual-assets analysis

The instrument behind the capability claim in 1.1.2. It measures what the technical staff were capable of; it is deliberately not offered as evidence that the organisation was dysfunctional.

4.1.1 · What the measurement was

Window. Roughly six to eight years before this was written. The exact span is left out on purpose, and nothing in the argument turns on it.

Population. Everyone on the technical staff employed by the division at any point during that span — not everyone employed on a single fixed date. Two strata:

  • Group A — scientists and engineers (control, mechanical, data science): n = 14
  • Group B — software developers: n = 9
  • Total: n = 23

Coding rule. Each person was assigned their current position, then classified:

ClassDefinition
Notable exitLeft, and holds a mid-to-high-level position judged against international academic or industrial standards
Other exitLeft, position not meeting that bar
RetainedStill with the division

For Group B the bar was set differently and deliberately: securing an international developer role from inside a sanctioned labour market was itself treated as the notable threshold, on the reasoning that the filter is severe independent of the role’s seniority.

4.1.4 · Notes on the coding

The classification is deliberately conservative, so 48% is a floor rather than an estimate. Three placements that many readers would count as notable were coded as other exits: two positions at a North American research university and one funded doctoral and postdoctoral appointment in Europe. Coding them the other way would put the notable share above 60%. The conservative direction was chosen so that the finding survives an unfriendly reader recoding the marginal cases.

The judgement of capability is not mine. Each notable placement represents a decision by an independent third party — an admissions committee, a hiring manager, a research group with its own funding at stake — none of whom had any interest in this division’s internal politics. That is what the measurement is for: it substitutes external market judgement for the internal assessment that the organisation could not produce credibly.
