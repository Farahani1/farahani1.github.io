Scope and attribution

Anonymization. The organization is described by size and sector only. Nobody is named. That includes the intellectual-assets analysis, where the source material names the people who left and where they went; in 4.1 the destinations are reported as categories with counts. No performance figures, financial figures, customer identities or proprietary product details appear anywhere in this entry, and no internal document is reproduced in full.

Attribution. The diagnosis, the framework, the PRD wedge, the authoring process, the survey design and the phased strategy are my own work. The role and lifecycle model in 2.4.2 is a colleague’s contribution, introduced here rather than claimed. Several of the source artifacts are signed “Systems Design and Engineering Team”, of which I was a member and the person responsible for this change; where this document says “we”, that team is who is meant.

What is not claimed. That the intervention succeeded. 3.1 says plainly that it was half executed, and where it stopped.
