SERVICES

There are three depths you can bring me in at: decide, design, build.

You do not need to know which one you need. We can figure that out together.

1 · AI Systems Advisory

Deciding whether it is worth building

You are considering an AI initiative, preparing to commit a budget, or reviewing a proposal from an external contractor.

I look at the problem, the proposed solution, the data and processes behind it, and the organization’s ability to use and maintain what gets built.

The key questions are often simple:

  • Is the data good enough?

  • Does AI actually add value here?

  • What happens when the system is wrong?

  • Who will own and operate it?

  • Can the organization support it?

For outsourced work, I can also help define the scope, responsibilities, acceptance criteria, and what “done” means.

What I commit to

  • A written assessment with the key risks and the path I would take in your position.

  • A clear recommendation, rather than a menu of possibilities.

  • An honest account of what is still unknown or missing.

  • Technical specifications and acceptance criteria when an external team is involved.

  • Advice that stands on its own, whether or not you hire me to build the system.

I do not advise on: your market, sales strategy, or product-market fit. Those are different problems.

2 · AI Systems Design

Turning the decision into a buildable system

You have decided to build. Now the important question is: what should the system actually be?

I define the architecture, data requirements, component boundaries, technical approach, evaluation, and failure handling — including where not to use machine learning.

I also consider the system around the technology: workflows, ownership, maintenance, and how people will actually use the result.

What I commit to

  • A design that covers architecture, data, components, failure handling, evaluation, and implementation sequence.

  • Success criteria defined before the build starts.

  • A design another development team can build from.

  • A system sized for the organization that has to run it.

  • A prototype where the most uncertain part needs to be tested.

  • A clear recommendation when part of the solution does not need machine learning — even when that reduces my scope.

3 · AI Systems Engineering

Building it into something usable

You need the system built, tested, and deployed.

I build the parts end to end: data pipelines, models, APIs, services, evaluation, containerization, deployment, and integration.

When the project needs an interface, I build that too. The model is not the product; the usable system is.

I then hand it over properly.

What I commit to

  • Working software in your infrastructure, not just a prototype.

  • Source code, tests, evaluation results, deployment configuration, and documentation as part of the delivery.

  • A handover your team can actually use to operate and maintain the system.

  • Clear boundaries around what I can and cannot responsibly take on.

  • Containerized ML services, edge and embedded integrations, and end-to-end AI systems at the scale most organizations need — but not heavy multi-region distributed infrastructure.

  • If I stop believing the project can succeed, I will say so, even after work has started.
